• A session with no sets is never saved: finishing it just cancels it.
• Switching language mid-session loses nothing: typed weight and reps, the exercise and the screen come back as they were.
• Session names follow the language, e.g. "5×5 for beginners · Day A".
• Body measurements must make sense together: waist below height, fat/muscle/water percentages within 100%, weight and height that fit.
• A session that runs past midnight splits its calories between the two days by when the sets were done.
• "Cancel" in the plan dialog puts the programme back as it was.
