• Before a session: "What's today's workout?" with your next day and its time, muscle readiness and your last session. If a muscle is still resting it tells you and moves it to the end.
• During a session: a thin bar with the timer and Finish, a current-exercise card with arrows to the previous and next exercise, and "Change exercise" opens a sheet with your plan, search and "+ New exercise".
• "Set 3 of 5" with dots, and the save button stays at the bottom showing what it saves.
• "Fill from": today's suggestion, your last set, or the warm-up, in one tap.
• Exercise settings (load mode, machine and bar weight, rest) fold into one line.
• The log is grouped: one card per exercise with numbered sets, faded warm-ups, records marked, and approximate calories per exercise.
• Progress is now a list of your exercises: best number, how much it went up since the start, and a small trend line. Filter by muscle, search and sort.
• Tap an exercise to see its chart (4 weeks, 3 months or all) with records marked, a sentence on where you stand, and "Log a set now".
• Add exercise: equipment buttons, a line saying what was picked from the name, a preview, and a warning with "Use it" if you already have a similar exercise.
• "My exercises": every exercise grouped by muscle, your own on top, and confirm or fix the muscles the app guessed.
